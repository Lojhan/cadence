# Starter tablature exercises

The four `catalog:tab:*` entries in `packages/music/src/index.ts` are original
six-string **chord-voicing exercises**. They contain no lyrics, vocal melody,
instrumental hook, solo, recording, or copied published tablature. Their
headings and repeated chord columns give beginners a complete, player-paced
practice sequence. They are reductions, not note-for-note transcriptions.

| Entry | Basis | Rights and attribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Knockin' on Heaven's Door | G–D–Am / G–D–C chord loop, original open-chord voicings | Bob Dylan composition remains copyrighted; [official song page](https://www.bobdylan.com/songs/knockin-heavens-door/) identifies Ram's Horn Music. Only the commonplace chord progression and new voicing exercise are included. |
| Amazing Grace | Simplified chords from existing catalog entry 001 | John Newton hymn; source and CC0 harmony review in `imported-review.json`. New tab exercise. |
| When the Saints Go Marching In | Simplified chords from existing catalog entry 002 | Traditional tune; source and CC0 harmony review in `imported-review.json`. New tab exercise. |
| Jingle Bells | Simplified chords from existing catalog entry 066 | James Lord Pierpont song; source and CC0 harmony review in `imported-review.json`. New tab exercise. |

The chord loop alone is a musical building block; the [U.S. Copyright Office
compendium](https://copyright.gov/comp3/docs/compendium-122214.pdf) discusses
standard chord progressions and chord symbols as unprotectable material. This
record does **not** claim that Dylan's composition is public domain or that
Cadence may distribute its melody, lyrics, or published tab. Country-specific
review and physical guitar playthrough remain open before treating these
exercises as vetted instructional arrangements.
