    Interactive design preview · no microphone

    Unmute in the bottom dock to preview listening. The animated signal is simulated; this prototype never opens your microphone. Use the side chevrons or arrow keys to move between chords. Space mutes or unmutes. The timeline shows the current chord and the next two steps. After four idle seconds, the chord and fretboard stay visible, the timeline softens, and the controls fade away. Tap or move your pointer to reveal the controls.

    Production practice will advance only after a stable audio match and a fresh strum for repeated chords.
